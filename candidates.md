# The Internet in One Word — candidate scoring

Judge criteria, in order (RULES.txt): (1) a single word describing the whole
internet; (2) funny by itself, no explanation; (3) true about the internet as
a whole, not one app/site/mood; (4) surprising, not the first obvious word;
(5) lands in one beat: spelled right, instantly readable, short.
Scored 1–5 on Funny / Fits-whole / Surprising. Tie-break: laugh first, then
shorter (fewer bytes also wins the site's exact-tie rule).

Rejected outright: `internet`, `web`, `online` (generic); `porn` (shock for
its own sake does not count, per the judge brief); `404` (not a word);
`rabbit-hole` (hyphenated — not cleanly one word); `touchgrass`,
`receipts`-style slang that needs explaining.
